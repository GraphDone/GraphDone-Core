Projection coordinates belong to the display layer, never simulation nodes/fx/fy/position receipts. Projected cards open the original item's existing inspector; editing saved board positions explicitly returns to canonical layout first. Hold focus/page/filter through small pans and zooms; transform the accepted projection rather than rerouting every frame. Recompute live semantic memberships after actual data changes. Auto overview, manual Items, paging, Back, resize and reload need useful stable behavior.
